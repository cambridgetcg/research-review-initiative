# PLOS submission notes

Official pages checked **2026-09-14**. This is a practical reading for this
draft, not evidence that PLOS has agreed to receive or publish it. Recheck the
chosen journal and article route when a submission is actually prepared.

**Authorship.** Except for PLOS Medicine's separate criteria, PLOS requires a
substantial qualifying contribution, approval of the submitted version and
personal accountability. A group byline still needs an individual corresponding
author; contributions must be reported. For this packet, an accountable human
must genuinely perform/adopt qualifying work and approve the manuscript—not
merely lend a name. That role is presently unfilled. [PLOS authorship policy](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/authorship).

**AI attribution.** PLOS requires the tools, uses, affected materials and how
outputs were validated to be disclosed in Methods, or Acknowledgements when
there is no Methods section. Authors retain responsibility for accuracy,
attribution, sources and their interpretations. [AI Tools and Technologies policy](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/ethical-publishing-practice#loc-artificial-intelligence-tools-and-technologies).
PLOS's explanatory editorial also distinguishes AI assistance from accountable
human authorship. [Hoch and Clarke, 2025](https://journals.plos.org/plosbiology/article?id=10.1371/journal.pbio.3003274).

**Our disclosure record.** Before submission, fill one short table: tool/model
and date; actual operator; affected extraction/proof/code/text/figures; checks
performed; remaining limitations; adopting author. Do not replace substantive
AI involvement with a generic “language editing” statement. Keep same-controller
subagent checks separate from any actual external review.

**Journal peer review is a separate role.** PLOS does not allow AI tools to serve
as reviewers or decision-issuing editors. Any permitted assistance must be
disclosed; reviewers/editors must not upload confidential submissions to AI
services. Our external invitation is not a PLOS appointment. [Ethical Peer Review policy](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/ethical-publishing-practice#loc-artificial-intelligence-ai-tools-in-peer-review).

**A concern report differs from a manuscript.** PLOS accepts concerns from named,
pseudonymous or anonymous parties, requesting the article citation/DOI, specific
issues and competing interests. Therefore the manuscript-accountability point
above is not a claim that all concern notifications require named authorship.
[Ethical Publishing Practice overview](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/ethical-publishing-practice#loc-overview).

Current operational state: retain the technical packet privately, request a
scoped external check when a real sender is designated, and decide the intended
route afterward. No author identity, external reviewer, manuscript acceptance,
publication-ethics finding or outgoing correspondence is asserted by these notes.
