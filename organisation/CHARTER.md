# Proposed charter

Version 0.1 · 2026-09-14 · **Not adopted.** Working name and accountable roles
remain undecided. This is an operating proposal; it confers no legal status.

## Purpose and scope

Produce careful, attributed technical reviews of specific claims in mathematics,
machine learning and algorithms. Preserve the origin of an idea, the steps that
translate it into a finding, and later reproduction, criticism and correction.
Help authors and institutions inspect evidence and improve the research record.

Review may confirm a result, narrow it, identify a counterexample, find an
implementation discrepancy, or remain inconclusive. A publisher notice is a
lead whose scope must be read; a failed reproduction does not by itself show
misconduct. We do not infer motives or issue findings of fraud from technical
disagreement. Block signing, token balances, citations and address counts do
not establish scientific credibility.

## What independence means here

Disclose the initiative's organiser, funding, appointment powers and release
control. If it shares control or funding with Zerone, say so. Calling it a
separate initiative or giving it a new name does not change those facts.

Describe independence **for the particular review**, with four separate facts:

- Who controls each reviewer and their work; multiple agents or accounts under
  one operator are not independent reviewers.
- Relevant expertise, relationships with the authors, recent collaboration,
  institutional or financial interests, and any paid commission.
- What the reviewer actually examined, computed or proved, using which inputs
  and implementation. Re-running supplied code differs from writing a separate
  implementation, and neither alone independently checks the whole argument.
- Whether the reviewer could choose methods, retain disagreement and decline
  endorsement without the organiser or funder changing their assessment.

Use “externally checked” only for a named, completed scope undertaken by a
separately controlled reviewer with disclosed conflicts. Use a more limited
description when independence is partial or unresolved. Never imply an entire
report is independently verified because one artifact hash matched. Disclose
AI assistance and who operated and adopted it; do not invent human authorship,
human review or institutional endorsement.

## Minimal governance and funding

The proposed roles are **case lead**, **technical checker**, and **release
editor**. They are functions to fill, not people already appointed. One person
may perform several functions for an initial technical report, with the overlap
disclosed; that report cannot describe those overlapping checks as independent.
An author of the disputed analysis should not be the sole arbiter of its appeal.
If a non-involved editor is unavailable, disclose that limitation and seek a
separate review before claiming independent adjudication.

Record the budget source, any earmarked commission, payments and in-kind
support for each case. Scope and compensation are agreed before results:
pay for defined work and evidence, not accusation counts, adverse conclusions,
retractions, publicity or financial upside from a target. Sponsors may correct
factual disclosures but may not purchase a conclusion or erase dissent.
Disclose any material sponsor control over selection, release or resources.
If such control prevents an honest report, stop the commission and record why.

Selection criteria and reasons for declining a case should be recorded. A
bounded favourable or negative result may be released; inconclusive work should
be retained with its limitations. A founder controls only the powers actually
declared, not other contributors' attributed assessments.

## Evidence, authorship and accountability

Pin the exact paper version, proposition, assumptions, source locators and
artifact bytes. Keep original author contributions distinct from our extraction,
interpretation, implementation and new computation. Preserve lawful source
references rather than assuming access allows redistribution. Keep private
correspondence and personal information out of public bundles unless their
publication is agreed and necessary to understand the review.

Every release names its actual contributors, roles, conflicts, methods and
limits. Each contributor approves the exact version they adopt. Record that
approval and date; if a digital signature is supplied, preserve the signed
digest and how the key was associated with the contributor. A signature binds
an endorsement to bytes, not truth, expertise or independence. Unsigned or
unverified attribution must stay labelled as such.

## Reply, correction and appeal

Give the relevant authors a concrete statement, reproducible evidence and a
reasonable opportunity to respond before a critical report is sent onward or
published. They can correct errors and contest interpretation; they do not gain
an undisclosed veto over a sound review. Nonresponse is not confirmation.

Retain competing assessments. Corrections get new versions linked to the old
ones, with an explanation of what changed and which downstream conclusions
require another look. Do not silently replace prior public conclusions. Record
appeals, assign a non-involved reviewer where available, and preserve the
resulting reasons. Retract our own unsupported claims as readily as we correct
others. An institution's acknowledgement is not endorsement or an adjudication.
