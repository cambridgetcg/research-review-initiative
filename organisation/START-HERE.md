# A small review initiative — decisions and working packet

Prepared 2026-09-14. **Private operating draft, not an established organisation,
legal entity, independent editorial board, or authorised correspondence.**
No reviewer, institution, inbox or domain has been appointed or created.

Use [CHARTER.md](CHARTER.md) for the proposed commitments,
[WORKFLOW.md](WORKFLOW.md) for one review from intake through correction, and
[CASE-TEMPLATE.md](CASE-TEMPLATE.md) as the working record and report outline.
These drafts were prepared within the same operator-controlled assistant team;
their preparation is not external review or adoption of the charter.

## Three possible names

| Working name | Intended emphasis |
| --- | --- |
| **Proof & Replication Initiative** | A narrow, readable purpose for mathematics, ML and algorithms. |
| **Traceable Research Review** | Where a claim came from, what was checked, and what changed. |
| **Open Methods Review** | Methods and inspectable evidence, including negative results. |

No name, trademark, organisation, domain or social-handle availability has been
checked. The first is the suggested working label only. A new brand does not
establish independence from its founder, funder or Zerone.

## Decisions actually needed

1. **Form:** a disclosed research initiative first, or investigation of legal
   incorporation. If incorporation is wanted, choose jurisdiction and the
   intended activities before obtaining jurisdiction-specific advice. These
   files do not create legal status or answer incorporation requirements.
2. **Accountability:** who accepts responsibility for the work, controls the
   budget, approves release, and receives replies/corrections. Keep identities
   unfilled until those people or organisations actually accept the roles.
3. **First independent check:** identify a willing reviewer with relevant
   expertise and a different controller. Record their conflicts, exact scope
   and editorial freedom. Until then, publish only an honestly labelled
   organiser/team technical report if publication is otherwise chosen.
4. **First case and budget:** one narrow claim, a time/compute ceiling, an
   evidence packet, and a declared intended recipient. No payment depends on
   finding an error, obtaining publicity or eliciting institutional action.
5. **Name and correspondence:** choose a name after checking it; designate a
   real reply channel and sender before any outreach. No message is queued by
   this packet.

The first useful deliverable is one bounded technical review with reproducible
evidence, the author's response if supplied, and an explicitly scoped external
check. There is no need for a token, new consensus mechanism, accreditation
claim, or organisation-wide credibility score.

## Existing tools, with their limits

The repository's `tools/research-review/` journal can retain contributions,
relations, concerns and plural assessments. Its browser reader is
`/research/review/`. It checks internal hash consistency, not reviewer identity,
external time, independence or scientific truth. A DOI scan supplies leads;
it is not a fraud detector. Use these tools to support the case record, not to
replace accountable editorial decisions. Existing evidence packages can be
referenced by exact version and digest without republishing third-party papers.
